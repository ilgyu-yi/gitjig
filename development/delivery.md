# Delivery boundaries

Gitjig combines agent, local and repository capabilities under the existing [Mission](../MISSION.md). This document explains their composition and verification responsibilities for contributors. It does not create another product requirement or record a task's acceptance history. [Design](../DESIGN.md) owns the integrated structure; [Contracts C9](../CONTRACTS.md#c9-endpoints-and-whole-path-acceptance) owns endpoints and whole-path acceptance.

## Independently useful endpoints

| Capability | Declared endpoint | Conditions that remain separate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agent investigation | Attributed findings and explicit limits | Investigation does not claim implementation completion or project-write standing. |
| Agent change | Current review-ready candidate, or an explicitly delegated and confirmed native endpoint | Planning, checks, independent review and resolution establish scoped procedure standing. Actual permissions, delegation, repository rules and native outcome still govern publication, integration and merge. |
| Developer-local work | Inspected or prepared change, isolated check, current source/index correspondence, retained candidate or historical native-commit audit | Each result reports its own scope. Native Git retains commit, hooks and signing ownership. Correspondence is not test adequacy, review approval or a general human commit/push gate. |
| Repository administration | Observed identity and policy, retained preview, selected-setting application/readback, audit or reconciliation | Provider scopes differ. Selected matching values do not establish complete effective policy, causation or merge permission. Guidance-to-local-rule association remains an explicit developer decision. |
| Persistent goals | Durable criteria and independently reviewed goal completion | Goal turns, child/work allowances, procedure standing and confirmation of external effects remain distinct. |

Installation, setup and retained discovery must keep these entries reachable. Missing model or runtime configuration does not disable applicable model-free local and repository operations. Common configuration uses reviewed fields, with advanced inputs available where necessary. Show active versus saved-next-launch configuration and current versus future model choices.

The [interaction design](usability.md) describes input, progress, results and recovery across these endpoints. Product users should follow the [user guide](../docs/README.md), not contributor documents.

## Composition and recovery

Establish the actor, entry, required inputs, declared endpoint and recovery path for a change before adding mechanisms. Prefer existing Git, hosting and public Pi capabilities. A convenient command surface must not grant ordinary conversation direct project-write authority or bypass the controller's action admission.

Retain source identity, partial outcomes, native histories, pending obligations, consumed allowances and uncertain external outcomes across interruption and reopening. A view-only reopen does not dispatch work. Original recovery, grant renewal, definitely-unsent reauthorization and subsequent native preparation are separate decisions where the contracts require them. New IDs cannot clear an old uncertainty or replace its accounting.

Repeated use is part of the supported path. Identify recurring external steps and their cost, including native source synchronization and administrator policy transitions for restricted hosted merges. Verify a feasible repeated journey instead of treating a single successful operation as proof of a usable cycle. See [integration prerequisites](../docs/integrations.md#restricted-pr-merge-operator-prerequisites).

## Verification and support scope

A completion claim must name its endpoint and reach the changed consumer with representative healthy and adverse scenarios. Cover relevant interruption, partial effects, missing procedures, stale candidates, authority limits, unavailable prerequisites and unknown outcomes. Advertised combinations need evidence at their integration seams; independent component passes alone do not establish a coherent product.

Use the scoped lanes and explicit prerequisites in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md). Keep source, installed, controlled-procedure, actual-runtime and live-provider/service evidence distinct. Scripted model judgments and owned hosting fixtures do not qualify real model judgment, account permissions or service enforcement. A requirement that lacks evidence remains open; a support limit does not silently waive it.

Independent review assesses the actual candidate, remaining obligations, supported combinations and operating cost. Preserve valid scoped evidence while revalidating affected behavior. Put task decisions and verification summaries in the issue/PR, and raw evidence in ignored scratch storage. Current user-visible qualification limits belong in [Capabilities and limits](../docs/capabilities.md).

Product-governed development of gitjig itself requires applicable authorization and demonstrated end-to-end maturity. The development baseline does not establish dogfooding readiness. Native Git and external maintenance remain available independently; remote actions, paid services and personal installation changes require their applicable authority.
